# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis 🌾📊

## 📌 About the Project

This project was developed as part of my **Data Analytics Internship** conducted under the **VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions), Vodafone Idea (Vi), and AICTE** initiative.

The project focuses on analyzing agricultural performance across different **crops, states, and seasons**. The dataset contains information related to environmental conditions, farming practices, resource utilization, production, revenue, costs, profit, and disease/pest risk.

The main goal is to transform raw agricultural data into meaningful insights using **Python, data analysis, and visualization techniques**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze agricultural performance across different crops and seasons.
* Compare crop yield and production.
* Identify high-performing crops and states.
* Analyze revenue, cost, and profitability.
* Study the relationship between environmental factors and crop yield.
* Analyze irrigation methods and water usage.
* Examine water efficiency and its relationship with yield.
* Analyze disease and pest risk across crops.
* Identify patterns that can support better agricultural decision-making.

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 records and 28 attributes** covering various aspects of agricultural performance.

## 📈 Key Findings

Some important observations from the analysis include:

* The **average crop yield** in the dataset is approximately **5.27 tonnes/hectare**.
* **Sugarcane** showed the highest average yield and average profit among the analyzed crops.
* **Punjab** recorded the highest average yield among the states.
* **Kharif** showed the highest average profit among the seasons analyzed.
* Water efficiency showed a **strong positive association with crop yield** in the dataset.
* Yield also showed a positive relationship with production and profit.
* Market price showed a negative correlation with yield in this particular dataset.

> **Note:** Correlation indicates association between variables and does not necessarily imply causation.
